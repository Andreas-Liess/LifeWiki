# Writing Guidelines

## Required

At the beginning of every page, state:

- **Author:** Your name or a consistent pseudonym. The wiki owners must be able to identify you.
    
- **Confidence:** How confident you are that the information is correct. Use these 3 labels 
  (**Evidence, Experience, or Hearsay**)
    


## Best practice

Start with a short introduction summarising the main point of the page. Keep the rest clear, structured, and easy to read.
